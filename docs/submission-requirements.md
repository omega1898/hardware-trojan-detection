## Phase I award size

- DTRA STTR Phase I: up to $250,000 over 7 months, plus up to $6,500 Technical and Business Assistance (TABA); Phase II up to $1,500,000 over 24 months.
- Source: the [BW&CO topic page](https://www.bwcoconsulting.com/fod/dtr27tz02-nv002-ai-detection-of-hardware-trojans) and [BW&CO Release 2 summary](https://www.bwcoconsulting.com/programs/dow-sbir-sttr-release-2), attributed to the DTRA Release 2 instructions in DSIP. **Not yet confirmed against a DTRA source.**
- Per the BW&CO copy, the topic is not ITAR-restricted and lists CMMC Level 1.

## STTR partnering

STTR requires a formal partnership with a research institution, with a minimum share of the work done by each party. The exact percentages for this release are in the DSIP instructions and are **not yet confirmed here**.
